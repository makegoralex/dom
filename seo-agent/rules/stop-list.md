# Stop list

The system must never:

- invent Wordstat volume, rankings, traffic, leads or conversion forecasts;
- publish a topic that overlaps a protected commercial landing page without an explicit separation plan;
- create multiple articles for the same intent by swapping synonyms or years;
- auto-publish finance, legal, cadastral, safety or structural advice without human review;
- state a mortgage rate, programme condition, price, deadline or warranty without a dated source;
- copy competitor text, headings, tables or images;
- change an article date without a substantive update;
- fabricate an author, reviewer, quote, customer, project, measurement or case;
- present an AI-generated house as an Evtenia-built object;
- hide weak content behind keyword repetition, FAQ padding or city-name substitution;
- publish with missing title, description, canonical, category, author, CTA or internal links;
- publish when `/robots.txt`, sitemap inclusion or canonical behaviour is unknown;
- expose passwords, tokens, private customer data or internal estimates in content or logs;
- delete or redirect an existing indexed page without a reviewed migration map.
