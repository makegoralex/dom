# Editorial policy

## Purpose

Help a person choose a safe, affordable path to building a house and move naturally toward an Evtenia consultation. Search traffic is a means; a useful and verifiable answer is the product.

## Audience

- Families considering permanent residence near Penza.
- Owners or buyers of land who have not selected a technology or foundation.
- Buyers comparing a ready project with an individual design.
- Borrowers considering a mortgage for house construction.
- Customers who need related site, engineering and finishing services.

## Voice

- Clear Russian without bureaucratic phrasing or artificial enthusiasm.
- Explain trade-offs; do not declare one technology universally superior.
- Use local conditions only when supported by a source or Evtenia case.
- Separate an indicative range from a quote for a specific project.
- End with a relevant next step, not pressure.

## Evidence levels

1. `official`: law, government programme, standard, manufacturer documentation.
2. `company`: approved Evtenia process, estimate, measurement or completed case.
3. `expert`: named engineer, architect, mortgage specialist or cadastral engineer.
4. `market`: dated competitor observation used for structure, never copied as fact.

Every number and non-obvious claim must have an evidence level in the brief. Finance, law, safety and structural claims require `official` or a named `expert` review.

## Conversion rules

- One primary CTA per article, matching the reader’s current decision.
- Technology article -> technology/project consultation.
- Foundation or land article -> site and foundation assessment.
- Mortgage article -> mortgage eligibility consultation.
- Planning article -> select a project.
- CTA is a normal content block, never an H2/H3.

## Human review is mandatory for

- mortgage rates, eligibility and programme deadlines;
- legal, cadastral and registration procedures;
- structural calculations and foundation recommendations;
- prices, completion dates, warranties and promotional claims;
- any statement presented as Evtenia’s own experience.
