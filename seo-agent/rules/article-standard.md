# Reference article standard

## Inputs required before writing

- Cluster ID from `semantic-map.json`.
- Main query and supporting queries. Volume may be `null`, but must never be invented.
- Search intent and page owner.
- Two or three current organic competitors.
- Content gap: what the article adds beyond the current results.
- Approved Evtenia facts, examples and reviewer.
- Planned internal links and conversion action.

If these inputs are missing, create a brief with open questions; do not write a confident final article.

## Required structure

1. Unique title and one H1 with the cluster’s main phrase near the beginning where natural.
2. Two-to-four sentence answer immediately below the introduction.
3. Navigable contents for a long article.
4. H2/H3 sections that cover the cluster without repeating the same intent.
5. A decision table, checklist, calculation example or comparison when it materially helps.
6. Risks and “when this option does not fit”.
7. Evtenia practice or a clearly marked request for company evidence.
8. FAQ only for real remaining questions; answers must add information.
9. Relevant internal links inside the body.
10. Author, role, reviewer and visible updated date.
11. Primary CTA block and, if useful, related projects/services.

## SEO fields

- Latin lowercase slug with hyphens.
- Unique `title`; no forced 60-character truncation.
- Unique description up to 250 characters.
- Self-canonical.
- Exactly one H1.
- Descriptive alt text for informative images; empty alt for decorative images.
- Article/BlogPosting, BreadcrumbList, Person or Organization and ImageObject markup where the data exists.

## Images

- Prefer Evtenia projects, construction nodes, plans, diagrams and checklists.
- Every borrowed factual diagram needs a source and permission-compatible usage.
- AI images may illustrate a concept but must not impersonate a completed Evtenia object.
- Cover, in-body images and related-article cards must not use generic broken placeholders.

## Style red flags

Remove phrases such as “in today’s world”, “it is no secret”, “unique solution”, “ideal choice for everyone”, repeated conclusions, fake quotes, unsupported superlatives and keyword-stuffed headings.
