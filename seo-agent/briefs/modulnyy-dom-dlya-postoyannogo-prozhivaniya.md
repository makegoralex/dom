## Search intent

The reader wants to understand whether a modular house can be a full-time home, which disadvantages are real, and what documents, assemblies and systems to check before signing a contract.

## Content gap

Many pages answer with a generic list of pros and cons or a single wall thickness. This article treats year-round suitability as a whole-house engineering task: thermal envelope, joints, ventilation, heating, winter utilities, foundation, maintainability and acceptance. It does not use universal lifespan, price or schedule claims.
