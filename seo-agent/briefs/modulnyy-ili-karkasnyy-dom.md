## Search intent

The reader is comparing two apparently competing technologies and wants a practical recommendation on layout, site access, construction process, quality control, warmth, utilities, budget and future changes.

## Content gap

Many pages compare “modular” and “frame” as if they were mutually exclusive wall materials. In reality, a modular house may use a timber frame. The useful comparison is between volumetric modules completed mainly at production and a frame house assembled mainly on the plot. The article avoids universal claims about price, warmth and service life and gives a decision matrix instead.
