## Search intent

The reader wants to know whether a modular house advertised “with utilities” is actually ready to live in, which systems are assembled at the factory, what still has to be built on the plot and how to compare two offers without relying on the words “turnkey”.

## Content gap

Search results often describe one manufacturer’s package as if it were a market standard. The Evtenia article does not promise a universal package. It separates internal systems, inter-module connections, external utility routes, utility sources and commissioning, then gives the reader a comparison table and an acceptance checklist.
