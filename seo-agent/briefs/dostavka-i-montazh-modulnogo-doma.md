## Search intent

The reader needs to understand whether a finished module can reach the site, what must be prepared before the delivery date, which work is included in installation and why a price cannot be calculated from distance alone.

## Content gap

Competitor pages often reduce the answer to a generic list of road width, crane and foundation requirements, or publish unsupported fixed dimensions and prices. The Evtenia article connects the route, site, foundation, contract and acceptance in one decision process without inventing universal measurements.
