## Strategic correction

Foundation and mortgage are important but are not the centre of the factory. The first expanded map should cover:

1. Technology and house choice.
2. Modular houses nationwide: permanent living, package, delivery, price, reviews and limitations.
3. Frame houses and projects in Penza Oblast.
4. Project selection and planning.
5. Construction cost, package and stages.
6. Repair, exterior/interior finishing and engineering.
7. Land, surveying and preparation.
8. Septic systems, wells, doors, windows, fences and landscaping.
9. Mortgage, documents and registration.
10. Completed-object cases and decision mistakes.
